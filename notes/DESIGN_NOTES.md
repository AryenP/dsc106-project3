## Data decisions
- 9 CMIP6 models, r1i1p1f1, Amon tas: ACCESS-CM2, CanESM5, CMCC-ESM2, GFDL-ESM4, IPSL-CM6A-LR, MIROC6,
  MPI-ESM1-2-LR, MRI-ESM2-0, NorESM2-LM. All 9 have historical + all four Tier-1 SSPs for that member.
  Spread of climate sensitivity: CanESM5 runs hot, GFDL-ESM4 / MIROC6 / NorESM2-LM run cool.
- Baseline 1995–2014: last 20 years of the historical experiment, so no gap between "observed-like" and scenario data.
- Anomaly per model before pooling: models disagree on absolute temperature by several °C, much less on change.
- Median not mean: one hot model (CanESM5) would drag a mean.
- Decade means for map/ranking (removes year-to-year noise, makes the slider step meaningful);
  9-yr running mean for lines.
- 68 cities hand-picked for continental + latitudinal spread (incl. high-latitude Yakutsk, Murmansk, Anchorage,
  and tropical Lagos, Singapore, Manaus).
- Grid cells are 100–300 km → not urban heat island; stated on the page.

## Encoding decisions
- Map: Equal Earth projection (equal-area, so high-latitude warming isn't visually inflated like Mercator).
- Sequential single-family OrRd scale, FIXED domain 0–9 °C across every pathway & decade — changing the
  pathway changes the colours, never the legend. Alternative considered: per-view auto domain (rejected:
  SSP1-2.6 would look as alarming as SSP5-8.5).
- Ranking is a dot/range plot (position on a common scale = most accurate channel) with all 4 pathways per city;
  the grey bar between min and max pathway IS "how much is still a choice".
- Pathway colours ordered blue → gold → orange → dark red = low → high emissions (ordinal). Checked with a
  colour-vision-deficiency validator (adjacent pairs ΔE ≥ 10 deutan). Gold has low contrast on white, so every
  pathway is also labelled in text (legend, tooltip, end labels).
- Small multiples for comparison (colour = pathway, consistently), shared y-axis so panels compare by position.
  Alternative: one chart with all cities overlaid (rejected: 4 cities × 4 pathways = 16 lines).
- Model-range band only for the selected pathway (4 overlapping bands were unreadable).
- Global-mean dashed reference line in the ranking and every small multiple → "faster than the planet?" is answerable.

## Interaction decisions
- Pathway toggle + decade slider (with play) drive every view at once (linked views).
- Region filter = dynamic query (filters ranking, dims map dots, recomputes the headline sentence).
- Sort ranking by warming / pathway gap / latitude → latitude sort reveals Arctic amplification; gap sort shows
  where the choice of pathway matters most.
- Zoom/pan on map for crowded Europe / East Asia; grid-cell tooltip anywhere on the map.
- Click map dot or ranking row → add to comparison (max 4, oldest dropped); hover links map ↔ ranking.
- Dynamic headline sentence = annotation that updates with the controls.

## Findings to mention (checked in the data)
- 2090s, SSP5-8.5: typical city +4.4 °C vs global +3.8 °C; 48/68 cities warm faster than the planet.
- Pathway gap grows with latitude (exploratory/05). Siberian cities (Novosibirsk, Yakutsk) top the SSP5-8.5 ranking at ~+7 °C.
- Reykjavík and London barely warm under SSP1-2.6 (North Atlantic "warming hole").

## Process facts (fill in hours yourselves)
- Data access: CMIP6 Zarr on GCS via xarray/gcsfs (prep/fetch.py). Two models (MPI-ESM1-2-LR, NorESM2-LM)
