About the data

Source. Monthly near-surface air temperature (tas, table Amon) from 9 CMIP6 models, ensemble member r1i1p1f1, read from the public CMIP6 Zarr bucket on Google Cloud: . Each model contributes its historical run (1950–2014) and four ScenarioMIP runs (2015–2100):

  • SSP1-2.6: strong mitigation, net-zero CO₂ around mid-century.
  • SSP2-4.5: middle of the road, roughly current policies.
  • SSP3-7.0: regional rivalry, emissions roughly double by 2100.
  • SSP5-8.5: fossil-fuelled development, the highest-emissions pathway.

Transformations.

  1. Annual means of the monthly values.
  2. City values: bilinear interpolation of each model's grid to the city-centre coordinates. Model grids are 1–2.8° wide (roughly 100–300 km), so a "city" value describes the surrounding region. It does not capture urban heat islands.
  3. Anomalies: every model's temperature minus that model's own 1995–2014 average at the same place. Models disagree about absolute temperature by several degrees, but they agree much better about change, so we pool the changes.
  4. Ensemble: median across the 9 models; the band shows the minimum and maximum model.
  5. Map and ranking: decade means (e.g. 2050–2059) of the annual anomaly. The map grid is re-interpolated to a common 2.5° grid. Time-series lines: 9-year centred running mean, so year-to-year weather noise doesn't hide the trend.
  6. The colour scale and ranking axis are fixed across pathways and decades, so a colour or position always means the same number of degrees.

Preprocessing code: prep/fetch.py and prep/build.py in this repository.
